# M4: agents and test set

**Done when** (PLAN.md): the model choice is made from test-set results.

## Test set (built 2026-10-27)

- 98 8-Ks filed 2013 to 2019, 14 for each of 7 item types, picked with a fixed seed. Saved to R2 `agents/testset.jsonl` by the `agents-testset` workflow ([run 40589157029](https://github.com/poojan-9603/StockWatch/actions/runs/40589157029)).
- **Label leak (open question 1 from M3), decided: mask the headings.** Each 8-K names its own item and SEC title ("Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition"). The builder replaces both with "[heading removed]".
  - Before masking, all 98 of 98 showed their label's item number or a key phrase.
  - After masking, 17 do. None of those 17 are SEC headings; they're the body describing the event:
    - 14 of 14 bankruptcy filings mention bankruptcy or receivership
    - one 2.02 filing has a risk factor about "results of operations"
    - two 5.02 filings have company-written subheadings like "Appointment of New Chief Executive Officer"
  - So bankruptcy is an easy label, and that's fair: it's the text the model is supposed to read. Labeling exhibits instead would have meant reading press releases, which are longer and still name the event.
- Size: 480k characters in total (median 3,700 per document), roughly 120k tokens for one pass over the set.
